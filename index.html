Part I

規範對比Comparing the standards

MIL-STD-1275 是美軍地面車輛28V電力系統規範，ISO 7637 是民用車輛12V/24V系統的傳導性電氣干擾規範。兩者性質不同，但都涵蓋突波、尖峰、負載突降/啟動電壓凹陷等類似測試項目，可逐項對比。注意：現行 ISO 7637-2 只剩 Pulse 1、2a、2b、3a、3b，負載突降與啟動曲線已移到 ISO 16750-2，下面先分別介紹，再合併比較。 MIL-STD-1275 covers 28 V power in US military ground vehicles; ISO 7637 covers conducted electrical disturbances in 12 V / 24 V civilian vehicles. They differ in purpose but test similar things — surges, spikes, load dump and starting dips — so they can be compared item by item. Note that the current ISO 7637-2 keeps only pulses 1, 2a, 2b, 3a and 3b; load dump and the starting profile moved to ISO 16750-2. Each is introduced first, then compared together.

規範版本 Standard revision

本頁數值以現行版 MIL-STD-1275F（2022-09-07） 原文為準。初版（2026-08-28）依 E 版二手資料撰寫，2026-10-22 已校正尖峰能量、反接測試、冷車持續時間、突波測試條件等數值。每一項測試的波形與判定，可以到 MIL-STD-1275F 測試實驗室 互動試算。 Values on this page follow the original text of the current revision, MIL-STD-1275F (7 Sep 2022). The first version (2026-08-28) relied on secondary sources about revision E; on 2026-10-22 spike energy, the reverse-polarity test, cranking duration and surge test conditions were corrected. Every test's waveform and pass criterion can be explored in the MIL-STD-1275F Test Lab.

先認識兩份車規：ISO 7637-2 與 ISO 16750-2The two automotive standards: ISO 7637-2 and ISO 16750-2

兩份都是道路車輛的電氣標準，但分工不同。ISO 7637-2 只管電源線上的「快速瞬態」：感性負載切換、線束電感、開關彈跳造成的微秒到毫秒級脈衝，另外規定怎麼量設備自己發出的瞬態——MIL-STD-1275F 量測發出尖峰時引用的就是它（第三版）。ISO 16750-2 管的是「電氣負載環境」：工作電壓範圍、過電壓、疊加漣波、緩升緩降、瞬斷、重置、啟動曲線、負載突降、反接、接地偏移、開短路。2010 年起，原本在 ISO 7637-2 的負載突降（Pulse 5）與啟動曲線（Pulse 4）都移到 ISO 16750-2，所以兩份要一起看，才能跟 MIL-STD-1275 對比。 Both are road-vehicle electrical standards, with different jobs. ISO 7637-2 covers only fast transients on supply lines — microsecond-to-millisecond pulses from inductive load switching, harness inductance and contact bounce — plus how to measure the transients a device emits; MIL-STD-1275F cites it (third edition) for measuring emitted spikes. ISO 16750-2 covers the electrical load environment: supply voltage range, overvoltage, superimposed ripple, slow ramps, interruptions, reset, starting profile, load dump, reverse polarity, ground offset, open and short circuit. Since 2010 load dump (old pulse 5) and the starting profile (old pulse 4) live in ISO 16750-2, so both must be read together to compare with MIL-STD-1275.

對本案的意義 What it means for this design

跟 1275F 比，ISO 16750-2 有兩項反而更嚴：24V 啟動曲線掉到 6V（1275F 最低 12V），以及漣波到 10Vpp、掃到 200kHz（1275F 是 4Vpp、到 5kHz）。如果同一顆電源也要賣給 24V 民用或商用車隊，第二級的最低輸入電壓得從 10V 往下設計到 6V，而且升壓級控制迴路要承受更寬頻的漣波。（AI 推論） Against 1275F, ISO 16750-2 is stricter in two places: the 24 V starting profile drops to 6 V (1275F bottoms at 12 V), and ripple reaches 10 Vpp swept to 200 kHz (1275F: 4 Vpp up to 5 kHz). If the same supply is also sold into 24 V civilian or commercial fleets, the second stage must work down to 6 V instead of 10 V, and the boost control loop must tolerate much wider-band ripple. (AI inference)

結論：哪個比較困難？Verdict: which is harder?

MIL-STD-1275 整體上比 ISO 7637 更困難。Overall, MIL-STD-1275 is harder to meet than ISO 7637.

把 ISO 16750-2 一起納入後要修正一點：1275 難在突波（低阻抗源）、尖峰能量、反接與規範疊加的驗證負擔；但 24V 車規的啟動曲線（低到 6V）與漣波（10Vpp、到 200kHz）反而比 1275 嚴。同時要過兩邊的設計，輸入下限與迴路頻寬要以車規為準。（AI 推論） With ISO 16750-2 included, one correction: 1275 is harder on surge (low-impedance source), spike energy and reverse polarity, plus the combined qualification burden; but the 24 V automotive starting profile (down to 6 V) and ripple (10 Vpp, up to 200 kHz) are stricter than 1275. A design that must pass both should take its minimum input and loop bandwidth from the automotive side. (AI inference)

01

電壓能量等級更高Higher voltage and energy

±250V尖峰、100V/50ms突波；本案（54V/240W）MOSFET的SOA壓力（463W瞬間功耗）很重。要注意的是 24V 未抑制負載突降（ISO 16750-2 Test A）峰值可到 202V，比 1275 的 100V 還高，但源阻抗 1–8Ω、能量能用 TVS 吸收；1275 的突波來自低阻抗源，TVS 吸收不了，只能主動箝制——難在類型，不只在數字。（AI 推論） ±250 V spikes and 100 V / 50 ms surges put heavy SOA stress on this design's MOSFETs (463 W instantaneous). Note that the unsuppressed 24 V load dump (ISO 16750-2 test A) peaks at 202 V — above 1275's 100 V — but with 1–8 Ω source impedance a TVS can absorb it. 1275 surges come from a low-impedance source a TVS cannot absorb, so they must be actively clamped: the difficulty is the kind of event, not just the number. (AI inference)

02

反向極性要求更真實A more realistic reverse-polarity test

1275F 要求反接 33V 持續 5 分鐘不損壞，且反接電流不得超過正常工作電流，這比ISO7637 Pulse1的感性負載瞬態更難用簡單TVS二極體解決，往往需要主動理想二極體電路（如LM74700-Q1方案）。 1275F requires surviving 33 V reversed for 5 min with reverse current no higher than normal operating current. A simple TVS cannot handle that the way it handles ISO 7637 pulse 1; it usually takes an active ideal-diode circuit (an LM74700-Q1-style solution).

03

規範疊加驗證負擔Stacked qualification burden

必須與MIL-STD-461（EMI）、MIL-STD-810（環境）綁在一起驗證，整個專案驗證負擔遠高於車用ISO7637+CISPR25+ISO16750的組合，軍規測試方法更嚴格、允收裕度更小。 It is qualified together with MIL-STD-461 (EMI) and MIL-STD-810 (environment). The project's test burden is far above the automotive ISO 7637 + CISPR 25 + ISO 16750 set: stricter methods and smaller acceptance margins.

04

供應鏈與元件現成度Supply chain and part availability

ISO7637市場成熟，車規TVS、突波抑制IC選擇多、已針對40V以下量身優化；MIL-STD-1275需要的耐壓等級（100V/150V甚至更高）元件選擇少、成本高、業界經驗較少。 The ISO 7637 market is mature, with many automotive TVS and surge-stopper ICs tuned for under 40 V. The 100 V / 150 V-plus ratings MIL-STD-1275 needs have fewer parts, higher cost and less industry experience.

簡言之：如果只比較單一脈衝的電壓峰值，1275的±250V尖峰與100V/50ms突波在數字上就已經比ISO7637大多數脈衝更硬；再疊加軍規特有的反向電池、EMI（461）、環境（810）要求與驗證流程，MIL-STD-1275 對54V/240W電源的設計挑戰明顯高於單純滿足ISO7637的車用電源。 In short: on peak voltage alone, 1275's ±250 V spikes and 100 V / 50 ms surges already exceed most ISO 7637 pulses. Add the military reverse-battery, EMI (461) and environmental (810) requirements and their qualification, and a 54 V / 240 W supply for MIL-STD-1275 is clearly harder than an automotive supply that only meets ISO 7637.

Part II

設計指南Design guide

背景 Background

為什麼需要兩級式架構Why two stages

設計一個符合 MIL-STD-1275 規範（標稱輸入 28V）、輸出 240W、54V 的電源供應器，需要特殊的拓撲架構。由於輸出電壓（54V）遠高於最差情況下被箝制的輸入電壓（約 34V 至 40V），因此無法使用標準的純降壓型主動突波抑制器（active surge stopper）。 A 240 W, 54 V supply meeting MIL-STD-1275 (28 V nominal input) needs a particular topology. The 54 V output sits well above the worst-case clamped input (about 34–40 V), so a standard buck-only active surge stopper cannot be used.

架構必須結合「主動前端保護級」與「升壓型（Boost 或隔離式全橋/半橋）轉換器級」。 The architecture has to combine an active front-end protection stage with a step-up converter stage (boost, or an isolated full or half bridge).

拓撲選擇：隔離式 vs 非隔離式Topology: isolated vs non-isolated

在 240W 功率下，非隔離同步升壓轉換器效率高（~96%）、體積小、成本低。但由於 10V 輸入需要極高的升壓工作週期（約 81.5%）才能達到 54V，隔離式拓撲或特殊多相方案在技術上往往更具優勢。 At 240 W a non-isolated synchronous boost is efficient (~96 %), small and cheap. But reaching 54 V from 10 V needs a very high duty cycle (about 81.5 %), so an isolated topology or a multiphase design is often technically better.

方案二：分離式設計檢查清單（從零開始設計）Option 2: discrete design checklist

若從零開始以離散元件設計電路，元件須能承受 30A 冷車電流限制：Designing from discrete parts, every component must survive the 30 A cranking current:

A. 輸入電感選型Input inductor

  • 飽和電流（Isat）：至少選用 35A–40A 額定飽和電流的電感。若電感在 10V 冷車凹陷期間飽和，切換電流將失控飆升，觸發過電流保護（OCP）並導致 54V 輸出跌落。Saturation current (Isat): at least 35–40 A. If the inductor saturates in the 10 V dip, switch current runs away, trips over-current protection and the 54 V output drops.
  • 直流電阻（DCR）：保持極低（低於 2–3 mΩ），避免在長達 1 秒的冷車期間過熱。DC resistance (DCR): keep it very low (under 2–3 mΩ) so the inductor does not overheat during the crank.

B. 控制器選擇Controller

  • 選用支援 100% 工作週期／高頻同步整流的控制器，以在低輸入電壓下維持高效率。Choose a controller with 100 % duty-cycle support and high-frequency synchronous rectification to stay efficient at low input.
  • 使用具備外部閘極驅動電源或偏壓腳位的控制器（例如從已啟動的 54V 輸出回饋供電）。若控制器直接以下陷的 10V 輸入驅動 MOSFET 閘極，閘極驅動電壓可能過低，使 MOSFET 進入線性區而瞬間燒毀。Use a controller with an external gate-drive supply or bias pin (e.g. fed back from the running 54 V output). Driving gates from the sagging 10 V input can leave too little gate voltage, pushing MOSFETs into the linear region where they burn out.

C. 主動突波抑制器調校Surge-stopper settings

  • 前端突波抑制器（如 LT4363）的欠壓鎖定（UVLO）須設定在 10V 以下（例如 8V 或 9V），避免在冷車凹陷期間誤觸發關斷。Set the surge stopper's UVLO (e.g. LT4363) below 10 V — 8 V or 9 V — so it does not shut down during the cranking dip.
  • 串聯通路 MOSFET 須有極低的 RDS(on)，因為 30A 電流通過即使僅 10mΩ 的電阻，也會在冷車期間產生每顆 9W 的持續發熱。Series pass MOSFETs need very low RDS(on): 30 A through even 10 mΩ dissipates 9 W per device during cranking.

D. 輸入電容（大容量儲能）Input bulk capacitance

雖然 10V 凹陷由車輛電池支撐，高頻切換電流仍會使輸入電容承受壓力。應配置足量的聚合物鉭質或鋁電解電容組，以承受高漣波電流（10V 凹陷時估計 10A–15A RMS 漣波），避免電容乾涸或板上局部電壓下降。 The battery carries the 10 V dip, but high-frequency switching current still stresses the input capacitors. Fit enough polymer tantalum or aluminium electrolytic capacitance for the ripple current (an estimated 10–15 A RMS at 10 V) to avoid dry-out and local voltage droop.

設計須知重點清單Design takeaways

01

架構決策：輸出54V遠高於箝制後輸入電壓，不能用純buck型突波抑制器，必須是「主動保護前端 + 升壓/隔離轉換」兩級架構。Architecture: 54 V sits far above the clamped input, so a buck-only surge stopper cannot work; it must be two stages — active protection, then boost or isolated conversion.

02

輸入電壓範圍要涵蓋全部工況：不只是28V標稱，還要涵蓋10V冷車啟動與100V/±250V突波後被箝制的34-36V，第二級轉換器輸入窗口設計為約10V–36V。規範本身是 IES 12V（容差下 11V）持續 1 秒，加上冷車 16V 持續 30 秒；以 10V 設計留有餘裕。Cover every input condition: not just 28 V nominal but the 10 V crank and the 34–36 V clamped surge, so stage 2 spans about 10–36 V. The standard itself is IES 12 V (11 V with tolerance) for 1 s plus 16 V cranking for 30 s; designing at 10 V leaves margin.

03

突波級是最大熱設計挑戰：100V突波被箝制到36V時，MOSFET瞬間需消耗約463–487W（50ms），必須用多顆並聯MOSFET＋源極均流電阻＋故障計時電路防止SOA被超過而燒毀，或直接採用COTS突波保護模組規避風險。The surge stage is the hardest thermal problem: clamping 100 V to 36 V dissipates about 463–487 W for 50 ms. Use paralleled MOSFETs with ballast resistors and a fault timer to stay inside SOA, or avoid the risk with a COTS surge module.

04

冷車30A電流是關鍵設計基準：所有電感飽和電流、MOSFET RDS(on)、PCB線寬/銅重都要按30A（而非9.3A穩態）設計，否則冷車瞬間就會過熱或觸發OCP跳脫。散熱則要另外用 16V 冷車持續 30 秒（約 17A）評估，這段時間長到熱會傳到外殼。30 A cranking current is the design basis: size inductor saturation, MOSFET RDS(on) and PCB copper for 30 A, not the 9.3 A steady state, or the crank overheats or trips OCP. Check heatsinking separately at 16 V for 30 s (about 17 A) — long enough for heat to reach the enclosure.

05

控制器閘極驅動偏壓不能直接吃輸入電壓：10V冷車輸入若直接驅動MOSFET閘極，驅動電壓不足會讓MOSFET卡在線性區瞬間燒毀——必須用輸出回饋偏壓或獨立閘極驅動電源。Don't bias gate drive from the input: driving gates from a 10 V cranking input can leave MOSFETs stuck in the linear region and burning; bias from the output or a separate gate supply.

06

隔離 vs 非隔離的取捨：非隔離升壓效率高、體積小，但54V回流與28V共地，可能造成底盤接地基準偏移；240W這個功率等級建議採隔離架構，尤其若對接地乾淨度／安全隔離有要求。Isolated vs non-isolated: a non-isolated boost is efficient and small, but its 54 V return shares the 28 V ground and can shift the chassis reference; at 240 W isolation is recommended, especially where ground quality or safety isolation matters.

07

COTS vs 分離式設計是風險與客製化的權衡：COTS風險低、開發快但體積較大、成本較高；分離式設計可客製化但工程風險與驗證工作量大增。COTS vs discrete trades risk for customization: COTS is low-risk and fast but bigger and costlier; discrete can be tailored but adds engineering risk and test effort.

08

同時要滿足MIL-STD-461 EMI規範：前端需多級LC差模/共模濾波，且開關頻率諧波不能超出CE102傳導發射限值——EMI濾波設計要與突波保護級整合，不能事後補救。Meet MIL-STD-461 at the same time: multi-stage LC differential- and common-mode filtering up front, with switching harmonics inside the CE102 limits — design the EMI filter together with the surge stage, not as an afterthought.

09

PCB佈局是30A設計成敗關鍵：2oz銅、多層並聯、過孔堆疊、散熱過孔陣列、以及升壓開關迴路面積最小化，這些都直接影響溫升與EMI表現，不是後期可修補的細節。PCB layout decides a 30 A design: 2 oz copper, paralleled layers, via stitching, thermal via arrays and a minimal boost switching loop all drive temperature rise and EMI — they cannot be patched in later.
